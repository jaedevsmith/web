Zero-Credit Course Information

At the University of South Alabama, zero-credit courses appear on the transcript as pass/fail classes and are primarily used for research, internships, or thesis preparation rather than degree credit accumulation. Because they yield 0 credits, standard instructional hour calculations are not required.

  • Undergraduate Research: The UGR program (UGR 191, 291, 391, 491) records mentorship and research experiences as zero-credit-hour, pass/fail courses.
  • Graduate Internships: Psychology doctoral students in the CCP program may register for internship class CCP797 as a zero-credit course.
  • Thesis Preparation: History graduate students can enroll in HY 598 (Comp and Thesis Preparation) for continuous enrollment during comprehensive exam or thesis preparation.
  • School of Computing Internships: Students are required to register for a zero (0) credit internship course each semester they participate in the program.

Note: Courses not eligible for grade replacement include internships, clinical courses, and directed studies. Transfer policies generally exclude remedial or vocational non-credit coursework.
